# PlayFuel

**Simple food and drink guidance for athletes, before, during and after play.**

PlayFuel is a small, mobile-friendly website that helps players decide what to eat and drink around their sport. Pick your sport, how long you will play, your age group, and whether you eat veg or non-veg. In four taps you get a plan split into Before, During and After.

I built it as a learning project because most sports nutrition advice is either too technical or written for people who weigh every meal. Many players just want to know whether to have a banana or peanut butter bread, and what to drink.

## Features

- Eight sports: basketball, football, cricket, badminton, running, cycling, swimming and gym
- One question per screen, designed for phones first
- Before, During and After plan with separate Eat and Drink sections
- Indian foods and drinks such as poha, idli, dal, curd, nimbu pani, ORS and coconut water
- Amounts in household measures (katori, fist, palm) and grams, with estimated carbs and protein
- Healthier swaps for common choices, like whole wheat bread instead of white
- Switches for sun or heat and for match day
- Allergy filter for peanuts, dairy, eggs and gluten
- Urine colour hydration chart
- Short explanations of why carbs, protein and fluids matter

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single `index.html` file, hosted on Vercel. There is no backend and no data is collected or stored. Easy to edit: foods and sports live in simple lists at the top of the script.

## Important

PlayFuel gives **general guidance, not medical advice**. Carb and protein numbers are rounded estimates that vary by recipe and brand, and they have not yet been reviewed by a professional. It is not a diet plan. If you have a health condition or a serious allergy, or you train seriously, talk to a doctor or sports dietitian.

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. Nothing else is needed.
